What to pack

Connecticut in late December is cold — plan on warm layers.

  • Sleeping bag and pillow
  • Warm clothing — pants or jeans, shirts, sweatshirts, a coat, socks, long underwear, scarf, gloves
  • Sleepwear
  • Shoes — sneakers and shower sandals
  • Towel
  • Toiletries — soap or body wash, shampoo, toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Bible
  • Journal
  • Something to write with
  • Medication, if you take any
  • Water bottle or thermos
  • Flashlight
  • Umbrella (optional)

Participant guidelines

The following are not permitted at the retreat:

  • Knives or weapons of any kind
  • Gaming or music devices
  • Matches and lighters
  • Cigarettes and e-cigarettes
  • Illegal drugs and alcohol
  • Inappropriate attire

Students who bring a cell phone will hand it to their pastor at the start, and it is kept safe until the end of the retreat. Parents and students who need to reach each other can do so through a leader’s phone or the retreat center.

Serve

Come as a counselor

Counselors lead small groups, stay with students through the three days, and help carry the teaching into conversation. Applications include a short pastoral reference, and upperclassmen and graduates may also propose a seminar.
