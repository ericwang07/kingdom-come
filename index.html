While you’re there
What should I pack?

Warm layers, a sleeping bag and pillow, toiletries, a Bible and a journal. The full checklist, and the short list of what to leave at home, is on the Event Info page.

What’s the phone policy?

Students who bring a phone hand it to their pastor at the start, and it is kept safe until the end of the retreat. Parents and students who need to reach each other can do so through a leader’s phone or the retreat center.
